# Hardware Requirements

## Minimum System Specifications (CPU Inference / Testing)
- **Processor**: Intel Core i3 / AMD Ryzen 3 (Quad Core, 2.0 GHz or higher)
- **RAM**: 4 GB DDR4
- **Storage**: 2 GB free disk space (for dataset, weights, and results)
- **Camera**: Standard USB Webcam (720p / 30 FPS) or integrated laptop camera
- **Display**: 1366 x 768 minimum resolution

## Recommended Specifications (Real-Time Live Streaming & Training)
- **Processor**: Intel Core i5 / AMD Ryzen 5 or higher (Hexa-core / Octa-core)
- **GPU**: NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1650 / RTX 3060 or higher with 4GB+ VRAM (CUDA support)
- **RAM**: 8 GB - 16 GB DDR4/DDR5
- **Storage**: 10 GB SSD
- **Camera**: 1080p Full HD USB Camera (wide angle lens with close focal length for 3D printer nozzle tracking)
- **Lighting**: Consistent LED ring or strip lighting around the 3D printer build envelope
