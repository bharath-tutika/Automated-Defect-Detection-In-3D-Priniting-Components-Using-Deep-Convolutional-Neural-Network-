How to fix in Windows:
  1. Open Windows Settings (Win + I) ➔ Privacy & security ➔ Camera.
  2. Toggle "Let desktop apps access your camera" to ON.
  3. Close any app using the camera (Zoom, Teams, Camera app).
  4. Select your USB camera in the Device dropdown below and click Start Camera.
