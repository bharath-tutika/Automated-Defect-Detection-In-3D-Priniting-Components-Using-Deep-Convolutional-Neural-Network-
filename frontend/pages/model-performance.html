How to Train & Evaluate Your Custom Model

1. Prepare your dataset images and annotations in dataset/train and dataset/val according to dataset/data.yaml.

2. Execute the model training pipeline:

python training/train.py --epochs 50 --batch 16 --imgsz 640

3. Run evaluation to compute precision, recall, mAP, and generate the confusion matrix:

python training/evaluate.py

4. The generated weights will automatically be saved to models/trained/best.pt and metrics will appear in this dashboard.
